About these records

Zero is a reported value, not an estimated sale price. Estimated value is a separate field. Transfers include gifts, trusts, estate-related transactions and corrections as well as sales. Legal municipalities may differ from mailing cities. Older records may use broader transfer categories.

Source: Wisconsin Department of Revenue real estate transfer returns. Information is as reported; inclusion does not independently verify the transaction, every party or parcel, or the location.
