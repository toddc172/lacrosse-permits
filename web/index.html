About these records

These records come from the building and zoning reports provided to the Tribune. Building records cover the city of La Crosse; the county reports cover additional municipalities and may include zoning, health and land conservation permits. Each county record identifies its source department. Each row represents a permit. Multiple permits may describe work at the same property.

Locations use the report’s work address and parcel, rather than an owner’s mailing address. Municipalities derive from the parcel prefix using the Wisconsin Department of Revenue’s municipality table.

Costs describe the amounts recorded for individual permits. They do not establish a project’s final cost or whether work is complete. A city issue date does not establish the permit’s current status.
